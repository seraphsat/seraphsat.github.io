Team

The team started with students of the Premontrei Szent Norbert Gimnázium in Gödöllő. Three of us designed the Seraph board while still in secondary school, and all three of us now study at the BME Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Informatics. There were five of us in the CanSat competition. Both projects were mentored by Dr. István Seres.

The Seraph (HUNITY) board team

Máté Magó

Firmware on the board, testing the board and the whole system, operating the board in orbit and data analysis

BME VIK, electrical engineering (IMSc). He wrote the Seraph firmware and now works on operating the board in orbit and analysing the incoming measurements, including a method for recovering the true measurement times from the buffered telemetry. He presented the experiment at the MANT student seminar and at the H-SPACE 2026 conference. Alongside his studies he works in the avionics group of the BME Suborbitals rocket team. He has been into physics since 7th grade and entered every physics and maths competition he could.

Zsigmond Csaba

Testing the board and the whole system

BME VIK, electrical engineering. His physics teacher introduced him to the project. Programming is his hobby, and here he could also try building and soldering in practice.

Anna Horel

PCB design

BME VIK, computer engineering. She has always been interested in science, especially physics, and wanted to work in IT from an early age.

Mentor

Dr. István Seres

His main position is professor at the Hungarian University of Agriculture and Life Sciences (formerly Szent István University), and he also teaches an advanced physics elective at the Premontrei Szent Norbert Gimnázium in Gödöllő. He has worked with talented students for many years, regularly leads student technology projects, and popularises science with physics shows. He helps the team with the background knowledge needed for the measurements and the implementation, and when necessary he also organises funding and outside experts.
