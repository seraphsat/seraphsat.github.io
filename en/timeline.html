2026

  1. September 2026

    10-second mode

    From this month Seraph measures every 10 seconds (we had already tried this mode in two sessions in January). The evaluation of the data is in progress (Results).

  2. 21 May 2026

    Talk at the H-SPACE conference

    Máté Magó and Anna Horel presented the Seraph experiment at the H-SPACE 2026 conference in Budapest: the board's design and programming, the ground tests and the goal of the experiment. Conference website · Recording of our talk

2025

  1. 17 December 2025

    Seraph at the MANT student seminar

    Seraph was presented at the Space Age Student Seminar of the Hungarian Astronautical Society (MANT) and BME, in the programme item about HUNITY's student panels. The talk was given by Máté Magó and Zsigmond Csaba. Event page (in Hungarian)

  2. 23 December 2025

    The first measurements arrive

    On this day the first measurement data from Seraph arrived from orbit.

  3. 28 November 2025

    HUNITY is launched

    The satellite reached a sun-synchronous orbit; its NORAD ID is 66670. Details on the HUNITY page.

  4. 7 June 2025

    Alumni day

    We gave a talk about our projects at our school's alumni day.

  5. 23–25 April 2025

    Talk at the OTDK

    We presented the TDK paper based on the CanSat project in the Technical Sciences Section of the 37th OTDK, at the University of Debrecen, Faculty of Engineering. Section page (in Hungarian)

2024

  1. 12 December 2024

    Cold test down to −35.5 °C

    We tested the board in an industrial freezer in a MATE laboratory; the lowest temperature was −35.5 °C. Thanks to the university for the opportunity! With the light sensor we also proved that the light in the fridge really does turn off when you close the door.

  2. 10 December 2024

    We announced the HUNITY project

    We had been working on it for months, and this is when we showed it: a 45 × 30 × 3 mm board of our own design, which now measures temperature and light in space on board the HUNITY PocketQube satellite.

  3. 20 November 2024

    2nd place at the MATE TDK conference

    With the TDK paper written from the CanSat project we won 2nd place in the Materials, Industrial Technology and Mechatronics section of MATE's institutional Scientific Students' Associations conference, which qualified us for the OTDK. MATE news (in Hungarian)

  4. 19 June 2024

    Student council day

    We gave a talk about our projects at our school's student council day.

  5. 15 May 2024

    ESERO Hungary opening

    In May we presented our CanSat project at the opening of ESERO Hungary, together with our school's CanSat team from the previous year, PreSat. Thank you for the invitation!

    Photo: Design Terminal

  6. 6 April 2024

    Scientific Award at the final

    At the CanSat Hungary final we received the Scientific Award (a special prize). Report on the final by Űrvilág (in Hungarian)

  7. 27 March 2024

    Getting ready for the final, and the image-analysis AI is done

    We assembled the main board, tested the camera system and checked the structure with test throws in Palotakert. On the same day we also wrote that the artificial intelligence was ready: it estimates how much of an image is covered by natural green.

  8. 25 March 2024

    PLR submitted, and a team T-shirt

    We submitted the Pre-Launch Report on time. The last day was a rush, but there was still time for a final sensor test and a drop test with the parachute. We soldered the main board together that week, so we could already take measurements during the drop. We also had team T-shirts made for the final.

  9. 9 March 2024

    We are finalists

    At the meeting codenamed MWAIN ("Meeting without an interesting name") we received the finalist medal, which meant we had qualified for the CanSat Hungary final.
