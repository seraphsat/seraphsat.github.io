The HUNITY (NMHH-1) satellite

The satellite Data decoder

Seraph data decoder

We wrote this small app to quickly visualise Seraph's data. Paste the board's hexadecimal data lines one per line, and the app plots the curves of the two thermometers and the light sensor. The graphs can be shown as points or lines.

The app runs on an external server, so the first load can take a while. If it does not appear in the frame below, open it in a new tab.
